Commercial FAQ

Do we need to write a script for our brand video?

Not necessarily! If you have a script, that's great. If not, we handle the creative direction. We can interview you or your staff, pull the best soundbites, and build the story organically in the editing room.

How do we prepare our business and staff for a shoot?

We try to be as non-disruptive as possible. For social media retainers, we just capture your team working naturally. For brand films, we will schedule a brief planning call to discuss staging, lighting, and any specific uniforms or branding you want featured.

Can you provide videos specifically for TikTok and Instagram Reels?

Yes, absolutely. Our Social Media Retainer package is designed exactly for this. We shoot vertically using our stabilized ground cameras and edit short, punchy, high-retention videos that perform incredibly well on social algorithms.
