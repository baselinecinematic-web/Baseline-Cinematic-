3 ways to put your video to work

A great video only pays off when people see it. These three take about ten minutes and keep working for months.

  1. Add it to your Google Business Profile

    In your profile, choose Add photos & videos, then upload a short clip. Video helps your listing stand out in Maps and local search.

    Google takes clips up to 30 seconds, so use a vertical reel or a short trim of the master.

  2. Put it at the top of your website

    Place the master cut on your homepage, above the fold, where visitors land. A muted, looping clip works well as a hero background.

    Realtors: put the MLS version in the listing's virtual tour field.

  3. Post a reel and pin it

    Post a vertical reel to Facebook and Instagram, then pin it to the top of your profile so it's the first thing new visitors see.

    Tag @baselinecinematic and we'll share it too.
