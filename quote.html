Before you book

Turnaround, weather, revisions and the rest of the fine print.

How long does it take to receive the final edited video?

Because we handle all of our editing, motion tracking, and color enhancement in-house, our typical turnaround time for standard promotional and real estate projects is 1 to 2 business days. We ensure every frame is polished to perfection before delivering the final product.

Do I get an unbranded version for the MLS?

Yes. Every real estate video comes with an unbranded version, with no logos or contact details, so it's ready to post on the MLS, listing sites, or any social media platform.

What happens if it rains or is too windy on the day of the shoot?

Central Illinois weather can be unpredictable, but we never compromise on safety or the quality of your footage. If the conditions aren't right for safe flying—such as high winds, rain, or poor visibility—we will simply work with you to reschedule the flight for the next clear weather window at no additional cost.

How many rounds of changes are included?

Your video includes two rounds of revisions for smaller changes, like trimming a clip, swapping a shot, or adjusting the music. On top of that, our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee means that if you're not happy with the final video, we'll re-edit it at no additional charge.

Is the music in my video licensed?

Yes. All of our music comes from a professional licensed music library, so your video is cleared to use on your listing, website, and social media.

Do you charge a travel fee?

Shoots within 50 miles of Pekin and Peoria have no travel fee. Beyond that, it's $50 for 50–75 miles, $100 for 75–100 miles, and a custom quote for anything over 100 miles. You'll always know the travel fee before you book.

How do I pay? Is a deposit required?

We accept PayPal, Cash App, and all major credit cards. Larger projects, such as brand films and commercial campaigns, require a 40% deposit to reserve your date.
