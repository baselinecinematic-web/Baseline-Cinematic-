Why We Started

Baseline Cinematic began with Michael editing short videos to help our church, Gethsemane Church, reach more people. Filming those events with a drone showed us how many great local businesses, churches and homes go unnoticed online, simply because no one ever showed them well. So we built a company to change that for Central Illinois. Every project still gets the same care as that first one: making sure the people and places around us finally get the attention they deserve.
