Peoria-area questions

Do you charge travel to come to Peoria?

No. Peoria, East Peoria, Morton, Washington and Bloomington-Normal are all inside our 50-mile radius from Pekin, so there's no travel fee.

Can you fly a drone near Peoria International Airport?

Often, yes, with permission. Part of Peoria is inside the airport's controlled airspace. As FAA Part 107 certified pilots we request authorization before the shoot, and if a location can't be approved we'll tell you before you book.

How fast will I get my video?

Most real estate and promotional videos are delivered in 1–2 business days. If you need it sooner, rush delivery gets you the finished film within 24 hours.

What if the weather turns on shoot day?

Central Illinois wind and rain happen. If it isn't safe to fly, we move the shoot to the next clear window at no extra cost.
